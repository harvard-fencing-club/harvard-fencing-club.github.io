# Handoff

Jekyll site for Harvard Fencing Club on GitHub Pages (no backend, no plugins beyond GitHub Pages defaults). Keep it minimal-text, crimson-focused, low-scroll, and easy to edit. Preview with `jekyll serve` (localhost:4000).

Pages: `index.html` (practice times + slideshow), `events.html`, `people.html` (leadership + outreach; the "People" tab, formerly Team; `redirects/team.html` forwards old links), `gallery.html`, `play.html` (fencing game), `resources.html` (links + FAQ), `donate.html` (Harvard Athletics giving form, fund "Club Fencing"; checks to Friends of Harvard; the 25¢ match was stated by the user and isn't on Harvard's pages). Shared shell: `_layouts/default.html`, `_includes/header.html`, `_includes/footer.html`; nav lives in `_config.yml`. Club details: `assets/js/content.js`. Styles: `assets/css/site.css`. Scripts: `assets/js/site.js` (every page: helpers exposed as `window.HFC`, menu, theme, contact links) plus one script per page named in front matter `scripts: [...]` (`home`, `events`, `people`, `gallery`, `resources`, `game`; `photos` = generated data). Code is Prettier-formatted (print width 110). Images in `assets/images/`; photo tool in `tools/`. Keep links relative (works under a repo subpath).

Photos: `assets/js/photos.js` (generated by `python tools/update-photos.py`) holds two Drive folder lists: `home` (20-30 picks for the home slideshow) and `gallery` (everything; the script also groups consecutive look-alike shots into ~95 "collections" with Pillow, sharpest shot first as the cover, then applies the user's manual `MERGES` (photo-ID based, so they survive reruns), then reverses the order so the newest collections show first (`NEWEST_FIRST`), then applies manual `SPLITS`. Currently 602 photos in 69 collections. `gallery.html` shows one tile per collection with a count badge; the viewer's left/right moves between collections and a thumbnail strip / up-down shows the similar shots, images via `lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/ID=wN`). Folder links live in `FOLDERS` at the top of the script; rerun it after the folders change. Videos are skipped. No photo files in the repo except team/event photos in `assets/images/`. Home slideshow uses `HOME_PICKS` in `tools/update-photos.py` (20 hand-picked IDs chosen by the user with a temporary picker; tall photos keep their upper portion, `HOME_FOCUS` overrides framing) until it's emptied in favour of a curated home folder; photos credited "Photos © Kevin Cho/Michel Tao 2026" under the slideshow. Do not identify photo subjects or assign them to leadership cards.

Game (`assets/js/game.js`): Sabre / Foil / Épée picker above the levels (`WEAPONS` table holds each weapon's rules, blade angles, parry positions; rules documented at the top of the file). Lockouts are real values x0.7 (sabre 120, foil 210, épée 32 ms). Sabre: on a double, a recent parry wins ("Riposte!"), else the earlier attack start wins, where an unbroken advance into a lunge counts as one attack from the start of the advance (a pause/retreat > 120 ms breaks it); attacks starting within 120 ms: on the first action in the box after "Allez" only, the lunge launched >40 ms first wins ("Attack in prep!"), otherwise "Simultaneous" (the first action ends when either fencer retreats, pauses after advancing, parries, or a lunge falls short); parried or short attacks lose priority. Foil (user-specified): same right of way, but box lunges within 90 ms are simultaneous and outside the box attacks must start within 50 ms (rare simultaneous); torso-only target, a touch reaching only the white arm is "Off target" (white lamp), stops play with no point even if the counter-attack lands, and play restarts at en garde distance from that spot on the strip. Épée: no right of way, whole body incl. the hand (longer reach), doubles score both, a double at 4-4 is annulled. Kit: sabre silver lamé torso+arms, silver mask, lamé bib, upright blade (72°/45° moving), knuckle-bow guard, parry raises blade; foil grey lamé torso only, white arms, black mask, thin blade at ~22° with side-coloured tape on its top sixth, small bell, button tip, quarte parry; épée all white, black mask, thicker blade ~10°, large bell, sixte parry. Off the strip = front foot back past the red line. Re-check rule changes with scripted scenarios (sabre: advance vs counter-attack, box, lockout, pause, retreat, parry-riposte, short attack; foil: box windows, outside-box timing, off target + counter, restart spot; épée: doubles, 4-4, lockout, hand hits).

Game levels: Novice, Learner, Club, Pro, Olympian (`LEVELS` in game.js; Learner/Club are blends 0.50/0.82 between Novice and Pro). Balanced by simulating level-vs-level bouts (both fencers driven by `runAi`) and against scripted skilled humans (a steady one and one with varied reactions/pauses), per weapon: each level wins ~90% of touches against the level below (épée lower, ~60-85%, because doubles give both a point); the skilled human wins about the same share against each level in all three weapons (steady: Club ~80%, Pro ~25-30% of bouts); Olympian (`smart`, ~45 ms reactions) is unbeaten. Per-weapon AI knobs: foil `boxPatience` (extra wind-up on the first action), épée `strikeInto` (distance to hit an advance). Club/Pro advance ~90% of the time; the AI never retreats off the strip. Rebalance with the same simulations if AI parameters or rules change.
