# Handoff

Jekyll site for Harvard Fencing Club on GitHub Pages (no backend, no plugins beyond GitHub Pages defaults). Keep it minimal-text, crimson-focused, low-scroll, and easy to edit. Preview with `jekyll serve` (localhost:4000).

Pages: `index.html` (practice times + slideshow), `events.html`, `people.html` (leadership + outreach; the "People" tab, formerly Team; `redirects/team.html` forwards old links), `gallery.html`, `play.html` (fencing game), `resources.html` (links + FAQ), `donate.html` (Harvard Athletics giving form, fund "Club Fencing"; checks to Friends of Harvard; the 25¢ match was stated by the user and isn't on Harvard's pages). Shared shell: `_layouts/default.html`, `_includes/header.html`, `_includes/footer.html`; nav lives in `_config.yml`. Club details: `assets/js/content.js`. Styles: `assets/css/site.css`. Scripts: `assets/js/site.js` (every page: helpers exposed as `window.HFC`, menu, theme, contact links) plus one script per page named in front matter `scripts: [...]` (`home`, `events`, `people`, `gallery`, `resources`, `game`; `photos` = generated data). Code is Prettier-formatted (print width 110). Images in `assets/images/`; photo tool in `tools/`. Keep links relative (works under a repo subpath).

Contact: harvardfencingclub@gmail.com, Instagram @theharvardfencingclub, and the WhatsApp group (`whatsapp` in content.js; button on Home). Activities include Crimson Club Open Bouting (Thursdays 7–10 PM; shown on the home schedule) and the NEIFC Big One.

Photos: `assets/js/photos.js` (generated by `python tools/update-photos.py`) holds two Drive folder lists: `home` (20-30 picks for the home slideshow) and `gallery` (everything; the script also groups consecutive look-alike shots into ~95 "collections" with Pillow, sharpest shot first as the cover. `gallery.html` shows one tile per collection with a count badge; the viewer's left/right moves between collections and a thumbnail strip / up-down shows the similar shots, images via `lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/ID=wN`). Folder links live in `FOLDERS` at the top of the script; rerun it after the folders change. Videos are skipped. No photo files in the repo except team/event photos in `assets/images/`. Home slideshow uses `HOME_PICKS` in `tools/update-photos.py` (25 hand-picked IDs: mostly action incl. 5+ sabre, ~30% aesthetic; tall photos keep their upper portion, `HOME_FOCUS` overrides framing) until it's emptied in favour of a curated home folder; photos credited "Photos © Michel Tao 2026" under the slideshow. Do not identify photo subjects or assign them to leadership cards.

Fonts: Graduate (uppercase wordmark, via `--title`), Barlow (body/headings), Barlow Semi Condensed bold (section labels, normal case), Geist Mono (small caps: event month, counters); loaded in `_layouts/default.html`. The home location line links to the Malkin Athletic Center on Google Maps (`practice.mapUrl`).

Do not invent dues, gear policies, competition eligibility, training promises, or club-specific founding history. Keep the broader Harvard fencing history distinct from today's student club.

Support current iOS Safari and Android Chrome (iOS 14+): keep `-webkit-backdrop-filter`, `vh` fallbacks before `svh`, and the `onMediaChange` helper. Check desktop/mobile layout, menu open/close, nav links, FAQ disclosure, slideshow navigation/pause, the game on keyboard and touch, and resource paths under a repository subpath. Google-hosted images need `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` (they 429 with a referrer). Reduced-motion users start with autoplay disabled. Update the essential static HTML fallback if practice/contact details change.

Harvard Club Sports website rules (soco.college.harvard.edu/clubsports/media-polices): keep the affiliation line "A student-run club sport organization at Harvard University" and the trademark statement (name and shield) in `_includes/footer.html`. Photos need signed releases (club's responsibility). The club should get Club Sports approval for site content and the "harvard" domain name.

Logo: official Harvard Athletics H shield (`assets/images/harvard-h-shield.png`, extracted unaltered from the Harvard Athletics Brand Identity Guide; the club reports permission to use it). The browser-tab icon is the crossed swords (`assets/images/favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png`). Never recolor the shield, outline, crop, or combine it. Clicking it toggles light/dark mode (saved in localStorage `hfc-theme`; light is the default). Dark palette lives in the `:root[data-theme="dark"]` block at the top of `assets/css/site.css`; use `--accent` for crimson text/borders (lighter in dark mode) and `--crimson` for solid fills.
