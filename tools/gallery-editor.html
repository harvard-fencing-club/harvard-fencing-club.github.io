How to use
  • Select photos by clicking them (click again to unselect, Esc clears). The bar at the bottom can make the selection a new collection, move it to a collection number, or make it the cover.
  • Drag a photo (or your whole selection) onto another collection to move it there, between two photos to place it, or onto a dashed gap between collections to start a new collection.
  • Drag ⠿ to move a whole collection: drop it on a gap to reorder, or onto another collection to merge them.
  • The first photo in a collection is its cover (the tile on the Gallery page). Hover a photo for: ⇤ make cover, ★ front page, ↻ rotate, ⤢ view large.
  • Every 10th collection (#1, #11, #21…) is a big tile automatically, so moving collections changes which are big. Always big makes another one big too. Collection numbers here follow the list; the Gallery may number a few differently because big tiles let small ones fill gaps.
  • The front page shuffles its photos on every visit, so their order here doesn't matter.
  • Save updates the site right away: refresh localhost:4000 to see it. Nothing goes live until it's committed and pushed.
