Donate

Thank you for supporting Harvard Fencing Club! Alumni and friends of the club can give online through Harvard's giving portal or by check.
Every donation helps more students share our love of fencing!

Click here to donate ↗

Harvard matches 25¢ for every $1 you give.

How to give?

  1. 1
    Click the button above to open Harvard's secure giving form.
  2. 2
    Under “Select a fund,” choose Club Fencing.
  3. 3
    Pick an amount and choose a timeframe.
  4. 4
    Add your details and submit. That's it!

Prefer a check? Make it payable to Friends of Harvard and write “Harvard Fencing Club” in the memo line. Email us for where to send it.

Where is my gift going?

  • EquipmentBlades, masks, whites, lamés, cords, bags, and repair tools so anyone can fence at no cost.
  • Access for everyoneOpening high-quality fencing to students of every background and experience level, including travel aid.
  • Growing the sportSharing our spirit of respect and sportsmanship through club events, on campus and beyond!

Good to know

  • Tax-deductibleGifts go to Harvard (President and Fellows of Harvard College, tax ID 04-2103580), a nonprofit, and are generally tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
  • Employer matchingMany employers match gifts too! Check the box on the form and send your employer's paperwork.
  • Questions?Harvard's giving office: 800-VERITAS · oga@harvard.edu. More at alumni.harvard.edu/giving.
